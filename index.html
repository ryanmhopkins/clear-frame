What can ClearFrame remove?
Metadata blocks such as EXIF (camera, dates, GPS, and thumbnails), XMP, IPTC, text, color profiles, and extra container data in JPEG, PNG, and WebP images. Some proprietary values can’t be decoded, but their blocks are still listed. Data hidden in pixels is outside the scanner’s scope.

      
Will my photo look the same?
Your image is never resized or recompressed, and your original is untouched. Removing orientation tags or color profiles can change how the copy displays, so color and display blocks are kept by default.

      
Do my photos ever leave my device?
No. Scanning and cleaning happen in this browser. Your photos aren’t uploaded or stored on a server.
